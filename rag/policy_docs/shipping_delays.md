# Shipping Delay Compensation

If a domestic order arrives more than **7 days** after its original estimated
arrival date, the customer is entitled to a **full waiver of the shipping fee**.
This applies to standard and expedited shipping tiers, but not to same-day
delivery. For delays beyond **14 days**, the customer may additionally claim a
**10% discount code** toward their next order. Delays caused by severe weather
are excluded from delay compensation.
