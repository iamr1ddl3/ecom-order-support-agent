# Subscription Cancellation

Subscriptions can be cancelled at any time from account settings and take effect
at the **end of the current billing cycle**. No partial-month refunds are issued
for early cancellation. Customers who cancel within **14 days** of their first-ever
subscription charge are eligible for a **one-time full refund** of that charge.
Re-subscribing after cancellation restores the previous plan price for **30 days**.
